# Origin AI Brand File

A portable brand reference for anyone (or any AI) producing content, design, or copy for Origin AI. Hand this to Claude Chat, ChatGPT, designers, contractors, or future hires. If they read this top to bottom, they'll produce work that lands on-brand without round-tripping with the team.

Current as of May 2026, post-website-rebuild.

## What the brand should feel like

Confident, calm, executive. Built for people who hold the budget and the risk. Restrained, photo-led, serious without being stiff. Substantive.

**Three opinions Origin holds out loud:**
1. AI executes. People decide. We build the line between them.
2. Stop paying for software that almost fits. Custom beats configured.
3. We own the outcome, not the deliverable. Done means it's running.

---

## What it shouldn't feel like

A tech startup. A SaaS landing page. A workshop business. An AI hype site. A McKinsey clone. A freelance dev shop. A tool reseller. Loud, cute, or hype-driven.

If a piece of writing or design could appear on five other AI consulting firms' sites unchanged, it's wrong for Origin.

---

## Voice and tone

**Voice.** Direct, plain-language, one step ahead of the reader. Origin sounds like a senior partner who has seen this play out before, has shipped the work themselves, and is telling you the truth without selling you.

**Tone moves.**
- Confident, not boastful
- Practical, not theoretical
- Specific, not abstract
- Calm, not urgent
- "We deliver", not "we explore"

**Sentence rhythm.** Short opening lines. Longer middle lines that explain. Short close. Avoid lists when prose works.

---

## Vocabulary

**Words to use:** strategy, roadmap, build, deploy, operate, deliver, working systems, ownership, governance, outcomes, results, discipline, structure, custom, senior, end to end, shipped, production, fit, judgment.

**Words to avoid:** unlock, leverage, transform, journey, seamless, cutting-edge, revolutionary, AI-powered, supercharge, paradigm, ideate, empower, synergy, holistic, disruptive, game-changing, next-generation.

**Words to avoid even harder:** agentic AI as a hype term, copilot as a hype term, "the future of work", any sentence containing both "AI" and "transformation".

---

## Hard rules

1. **No em dashes. Ever.** Mike's standing rule. Use commas, periods, or colons instead. The em dash is banned in all Origin AI output.

2. **No AI hype clichés.** Don't write "in today's fast-paced world" or "AI is changing everything" or "unlock the power of AI". If a sentence sounds like LinkedIn boilerplate, rewrite it.

3. **No stock photos with people in them.** Chad hates them. Acceptable imagery: abstract conceptual photography, warm desk objects, architectural details, light and shadow, paper textures, no faces or identifiable people. AI-generated abstract imagery in the brand palette is fine if it doesn't look generic.

4. **No bullet point overkill.** Most thoughts should be prose. Lists only when the items are genuinely parallel and would read worse as prose.

5. **No "we'll help you" speak.** Origin doesn't help. Origin builds, ships, owns. The brand is competent and accountable, not aspirational.

6. **The first person plural is "we" for Origin, never "I".** Even if Mike personally wrote the post.

## Color palette

The site lives on a dark, warm-leaning palette. Teal is the primary accent. Brass is the secondary accent reserved for the Build service area.

**Core colors**

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Page background | `#0E1311` | Default surface, deep warm dark |
| Cream / paper | `#F5F0E4` | Body text on dark, primary readable color |
| Teal (primary accent) | `#5DA89C` | Eyebrows, button outlines, link accents, key highlights |
| Brass (secondary accent) | `#C5A47E` | Reserved for /services/build context only |
| Strategy sage-brass | `#968560` | Reserved for /services/strategy context only |
| Muted cream | `rgba(245,240,228,0.66)` | Sub copy, body inside cards |
| Subtle text | `rgba(245,240,228,0.48)` | Captions, meta info, dates |
| Hairline | `rgba(245,240,228,0.08)` | Borders, dividers, card outlines |

**Forbidden colors:** bright corporate blue (the SaaS default), pure white as text on dark (use cream), bright red or orange (off-brand entirely).

## Typography

**Fonts in use:**
- **Inter Tight** for headlines and display text (weights 500 and 700)
- **Inter** for body copy (weights 400 and 500)
- **JetBrains Mono** for eyebrows, meta info, technical labels (all-caps with letter-spacing)

All three are free Google Fonts.

**Type hierarchy:**
- H1: clamp(36–60px), font weight 500, letter-spacing -0.018em
- H2: clamp(28–42px), font weight 500, letter-spacing -0.012em
- H3: 20–24px, font weight 500
- Body: 16–17px, line-height 1.55–1.68
- Eyebrow: 11px JetBrains Mono, ALL CAPS, letter-spacing 0.14–0.18em

The accent color word in a headline is teal. The rest is cream. Two-tone headlines (one phrase cream + one phrase muted) are a common pattern.

## Imagery

**Yes:** abstract conceptual photography, dark wood desks with warm directional light, paper textures, architectural details, olive branches and plants casting shadows, brass desk objects, out-of-focus tech (screens, code editors), data visualizations in the brand palette.

**No:** people in suits in glass towers, smiling diverse teams, handshakes, lightbulb-over-head metaphors, brain-with-circuits visuals, neon glow effects, anything purple or hot pink.

**For AI-generated imagery (Gemini, ChatGPT, Midjourney):** always prompt for dark/warm palette, no people, no faces, conceptual not literal. Match the existing six insights cover images on originai.ca/insights for visual reference.

## Examples of on-brand vs off-brand copy

## Social media specifics

For LinkedIn posts, X posts, and similar:

**Format.** Lead with a sentence, not a hook gimmick. No "1/" thread starters unless the thread genuinely has multiple parts. No emoji unless explicitly asked. No hashtag walls.

**Length.** Most posts are 100–250 words. Long-form posts go up to 600 words if the argument needs it. Don't pad.

**Tone.** Match the article voice. Confident, calm, specific. Reference Mike's three opinions where they apply. Avoid "thought leadership" framing.

**Visual.** If posting with an image, pull from existing originai.ca imagery, or generate something in the brand palette (dark, warm, teal accent, no people). Never use canned LinkedIn graphic templates.

**Links.** Always link back to the relevant article or service page on originai.ca. Use clean URLs, no UTM parameters in social copy.
